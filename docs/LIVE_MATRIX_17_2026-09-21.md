## Global execution rules

- `min_only` means measured value must be **at least** the card limit; `max_only` means **at most** the card limit.
- `ICC 2^n` means the static logic-input corner count where the model defines it. OE is included in `n` for 3-state parts. For dual parts, run the documented per-channel flow; a full-package 4-input 16-corner expansion is **UNSURE** because the model explicitly says one channel at a time.
- `ΔICC` is enabled only where the card/model supplies the mapped 500 µA maximum. Where the card names the quantity `ICCT`, it is recorded as the card's `ICCT → ΔICC` map; for the 2026-09-21 scale-wave parts the card symbol is `ΔICC` and `ICCT` is absent.
- `IOZ` is enabled only for an OE-controlled output. Open-drain `Y=Z` is not OE-gated IOZ.
- Common push-pull load grids used below are copied from the relevant card/model, not generalized beyond the listed values:
  - **1G08/1G32/1G14/1G00/1G02/1G04/1G86/RS2G08/RS2G32:** `VOH min`: −0.1 mA @ 1.65–5.5 V → VCC−0.1; −4 mA @ 1.65 V → 1.2 V; −8 mA @ 2.3 V → 1.9 V; −16 mA @ 3.0 V → 2.4 V; −24 mA @ 3.0 V → 2.3 V; −32 mA @ 4.5 V → 3.8 V. `VOL max`: 0.1 mA @ 1.65–5.5 V → 0.1 V; 4 mA @ 1.65 V → 0.45 V; 8 mA @ 2.3 V → 0.3 V; 16 mA @ 3.0 V → 0.4 V; 24 mA @ 3.0 V → 0.55 V; 32 mA @ 4.5 V → 0.55 V.
  - **1GT08/1GT32/1GT34:** `VOH min`: −0.1 mA @ 2.0–5.5 V → VCC−0.1; −8 mA @ 2.0 V → 1.6 V; −24 mA @ 3.3 V → 2.5 V; −32 mA @ 4.5/5.0/5.5 V → 3.8/4.2/4.8 V. `VOL max`: 0.1 mA @ 2.0–5.5 V → 0.1 V; 8 mA @ 2.0 V → 0.45 V; 24 mA @ 3.3 V → 0.55 V; 32 mA @ 4.5/5.0/5.5 V → 0.55/0.5/0.45 V.
  - **RS164:** `VOH min`: −0.1 mA @ 1.65–5.5 V → VCC−0.1; −4 mA @ 3.0 V → 1.2 V; −8 mA @ 3.3 V → 1.9 V; −10 mA @ 5.5 V → 3.8 V. `VOL max`: 0.1 mA @ 1.65–5.5 V → 0.1 V; 4 mA @ 3.0 V → 0.45 V; 8 mA @ 3.3 V → 0.4 V; 10 mA @ 5.5 V → 0.55 V.
  - **3-state 1G125/1G126:** same 1.65–5.5 V load grid as 1G08 family; test only while enabled.
- `UNSURE` means the requested source files do not assign that value or instrument connection; it is not a proposed limit.
